Subject: Author review — Research Methods in Sport and Exercise Science

Dear all,

We have prepared the author review edition of *Research Methods in Sport and Exercise Science: An Open Access Primer*. Please review your chapter's editorial changes and comments, make any further edits you would like us to consider, and check how the chapter appears in the proposed HTML and PDF editions.

You can read the draft book online at https://sportexercisemethods.org/ and download the whole-book PDF at https://sportexercisemethods.org/research-methods-ses.pdf. We are also supplying a dated PDF so that any annotations refer to a fixed version.

Your chapter's Word file retains the editors' tracked changes and comments. Please select Review > All Markup, keep Track Changes on for any new edits, and reply to the outstanding comments. You can accept or reject editorial changes individually in your returned copy. If you prefer to annotate the PDF, that is also welcome; please include your decisions on the existing Word comments and edits in an accompanying response list.

Please check the content as well as author names and affiliations, references, tables, figures, captions, equations, footnotes, links and any permissions information. The enclosed review instructions explain the files and how to return corrections. If your chapter has several authors, a coordinated response would be helpful.

When returning your response, please identify the dated review round and confirm whether all chapter authors agree it, whether you approve the chapter subject to the corrections supplied, and whether any questions remain. We will integrate agreed changes and provide updated proofs where needed before recording final sign-off.

The cover remains a placeholder, and the book is still a draft for author review.
